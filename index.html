Field notes

Quiet feet. Open cages.

Move

Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen. On desktop, use WASD or the arrow keys.

Free

Get close to a cage. Tap the big FREE button — or press E / Space.

Hide

Lantern light spots you. Duck into a leafy bush to vanish, then move when the guard turns.

Escape

Free everyone, then return to the glowing gate. Getting caught only rewinds the level.

Prefer steering by motion? Turn on Tilt controls. iPhone and iPad will ask for motion access. You can switch back anytime.
